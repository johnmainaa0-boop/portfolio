About

I’m studying for a BSc in Applied Statistics and Data Science. I’m at the beginning of the course, so most of what I do right now is learn the foundations and practise them.

I enjoy the idea that a messy table of numbers can answer a real question. I’m learning how to collect data, summarise it, draw it clearly and explain the result in plain language.

This site is where I’ll keep track of that progress. It is a learning record, and it will grow as I finish real work.
